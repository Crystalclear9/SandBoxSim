SandBoxSim modifications: evaluated one multiresolution subdivision, extracted
head/neck, lip and eye geometry, cropped and retargeted the neck, converted axes
and scale, split lip movement, generated scalp lookup samples, added vertex
tint regions, and exported compact mesh data. Runtime variants reshape width
and depth and reuse the game's own rig, blink, gaze and lip movement.
Current head export uses a uniform 0.90 scale instead of independently
compressing depth. Runtime retains gentle identity width/depth variation and
reconstructs transformed normals with the local deformation Jacobian; prior
individual jaw, cheek, brow, nose and lip-corner displacements were removed.

human-head.mesh.json is a derivative of this CC BY 4.0 asset. The project's
general software license does not replace this asset's license. Retain this
attribution and indicate further modifications when redistributing it.

Additional hand derivative (human-hand.mesh.json): extracted the authored palm,
finger and web topology; retargeted MCP/phalange positions with a smooth
coordinate field, fitted the wrist boundary to the game forearm, mirrored left
and right anatomy, recomputed normals, and generated 16-bone skin weights.
The nail surface is selected from the dorsal finger surface and shares its
skin weights. Exporter: tools/export_human_hands.py. The hand derivative is
also CC BY 4.0, with the same creator, source and attribution above.

Distance hand derivative (human-hand-lod.mesh.json): reduced from the above
licensed hand to 1,400 triangles using tools/export_hand_lod.py, with triangle
barycentric transfer of normals, UVs and skin weights. Runtime static open/grip
poses use the same articulation as the close hand. CC BY 4.0, same attribution.

Additional forearm derivative (human-forearm.mesh.json): extracted the authored
elbow and forearm from sculpt regions 12 and 102 with one multiresolution level;
cropped the sleeve/wrist openings, straightened the relaxed centreline,
retargeted length and wrist cross section, and recomputed normals. Runtime
mirrors geometry, normals and winding for the opposite side. CC BY 4.0, same
creator, source and attribution above. Exporter: tools/export_human_forearm.py.

Current hand derivative also joins the above forearm through a shared wrist
transition, preserving original hand vertex indices and nail partitions. The
near mesh has a single connected arm/hand skin; added forearm vertices bind to
the wrist compensation bone. Distant hand extraction retains only the original
hand region. The shared wrist transition uses the existing retargeted hand ring.
Join helper: tools/join_human_arm.py. Same CC BY 4.0 attribution applies.

Sculpt-normal derivative (human-head_normal.png): baked the source's second
multiresolution level onto a packed UV atlas for the first-level runtime head.
UV seams split runtime vertices; topology and triangle count remain unchanged.
Exporter/helper: tools/export_human_head.py and tools/bake_human_detail.py.
This normal atlas and human-head.mesh.json remain CC BY 4.0 under the above
Julien Kaspar / Blender Studio attribution.

Modifications: selected the head and neck, separated moving lips and eyes,
converted axes and scale, fitted neck roots, packed UVs, generated scalp
lookups and baked normal maps. Runtime identity variants, skin/hair materials,
gaze and mouth motion are supplied by SandBoxSim. The scan head keeps level-one
geometry; the feminine head bakes level-two detail onto level-one geometry.
Exporter: tools/export_human_variants.py; bake helper: tools/bake_human_detail.py.
These two independent head families are CC0, not covered by the CC BY 4.0
condition applicable to the earlier Julien Kaspar derivatives above.
