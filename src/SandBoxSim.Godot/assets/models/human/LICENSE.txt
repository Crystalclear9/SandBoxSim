human-head.mesh.json, human-scan.mesh.json and human-feminine.mesh.json:
CC0 HM08 base topology, male/female young macro target, source eye closure
expressions, fitted high-poly eyes and short01/short02/ponytail01 hair cards.
The historical filename human-scan identifies the neutral variant; it does not
claim that this replacement is a scan. Source UV skin textures are young
caucasian male, male2 and female from the CC0 MakeHuman system assets pack.
The packaged eye_albedo.png retains the original transparent cornea patch.

torso-cage.json: CC0 HM08 body with the existing male-young macro target.
SandBoxSim samples clothed chest/shoulder support sections, trims the A-pose
arms at the sleeve boundary, forms convex cloth envelopes and smooths them.
The JSON records source hashes, fitting scales and sampling dimensions.
This cage is used to fit garments; it is not a clinical anatomy model.

torso-feminine-cage.json is a derived radial cloth-support envelope of the
same MakeHuman CC0 HM08 base with caucasian-female-young.target. Input hashes,
sampling coordinates and reference scales are embedded in that asset.

trunk-masculine-cage.json and trunk-feminine-cage.json extend that CC0 recipe
to 113 x 128 thorax, lumbar and pelvic samples (0.74-1.30 client metres).
Plane intersections of the source body are convexly bridged for clothing,
including the gap between upper thighs; A-pose arms are excluded laterally.
Both files record verified inputs, scales and the sampling recipe. Runtime
cloth ease and body fitting are SandBoxSim artistic modifications.

Exact input filenames, URLs, sizes and SHA-256 are in head-sources.json.
Each runtime JSON also records the inputs actually used for that variant and
the checksums of its skin, hair and eye texture atlases.

SandBoxSim modifications: apply macro data, crop and fit the cervical ring,
subdivide source topology, preserve source UVs, export the original eyelid
closure displacement, partition mouth geometry with fixed attachment weights,
measure visible eye apertures and simplify the same head for distant actors.
Retarget hair cards to the head; retain the open-forehead ponytail;
apply independent actor blink/speech uniforms and age-dependent proportions.
The former Blender head meshes, separate eyelid overlays and three old head
normal atlases have been replaced. No MakeHuman application code is bundled.
Exporter: tools/export_resident_faces.py
Source fetcher: tools/fetch-human-assets.py

human-hand.mesh.json: extract palm/finger/web topology, smoothly retarget
phalanges, fit wrist, mirror anatomy, recompute normals, generate 16-bone
weights and retain dorsal nail partitions. Join the anatomical forearm through
a shared wrist transition, preserving original hand indices and nail surfaces.
Exporters: tools/export_human_hands.py and tools/join_human_arm.py.

human-hand-lod.mesh.json: reduce to 1,400 triangles, barycentrically transfer
attributes and preserve shared open/grip articulation.
Exporter: tools/export_hand_lod.py.

human-forearm.mesh.json: extract elbow/forearm sculpt regions 12 and 102, crop
sleeve/wrist, straighten centreline, retarget length/wrist and recompute normals.
Runtime mirrors geometry, normals and winding.
Runtime derivatives also fit the muscle volume with continuous transverse
scales, preserving the wrist/grip and hidden elbow boundary; near/far adult
presentation profiles share that fit and transformed surface normals.
Exporter: tools/export_human_forearm.py.

CONTINUOUS TROUSERS (CC0)
trousers-male.mesh.json and trousers-female.mesh.json derive from the
MakeHuman HM08 helper-tights clothing geometry and the verified young
male/female macro targets in head-sources.json. SandBoxSim retargets limb
centrelines, crops the waist and cuffs, removes disconnected helper parts,
fits hidden waist layers and boot overlap, subdivides, recalculates normals
and assigns waist, left/right thigh and left/right knee skinning. The cuffs
blend to the knee joints to retain boot contact. Each file records input
and support hashes. Exporter: tools/export_resident_trousers.py.
