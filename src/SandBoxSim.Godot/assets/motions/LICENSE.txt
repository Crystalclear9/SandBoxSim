The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

The database permits research and commercial product use, but prohibits
reselling the motion data directly, including in converted form.
This is not a CC0 license. Preserve this notice with the derived motion asset.

This asset contains body-relative upper/lower arm directions, wrist bases and a
chest frame derived from the ASF/AMC capture. Root travel and finger/thumb channels
are excluded. Game limb sizes, hand articulation, grip constraints and entry/
exit handling are provided by SandBoxSim's own presentation controller.
